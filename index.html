A simple 2-player boxing mind game.
Pick 3 moves, outguess your opponent.

Rules

Structure

A match has 3 rounds. Each round has 3 conflicts. Each conflict, both players secretly pick 3 moves in order. The moves clash beat-by-beat: your move 1 vs their move 1, move 2 vs move 2, move 3 vs move 3.

Speed

When both players attack in the same beat, the faster move lands and the slower one is interrupted (misses). Equal speed = both land (a trade). Jab is fastest, then straights/crosses/body shots, then hooks, then uppercuts, then the overhand cross (slowest but hardest-hitting).

Guard

Guard fully blocks light shots (jab, straight, cross, hooks, body shots). Heavy shots (uppercuts, overhand) still chip 1-3 damage through.

Sway

Swaying (either direction) cleanly dodges jab, straight, cross. Hooks, uppercuts, body shots and the overhand cross all catch a sway and land — hooks/uppercuts hit extra hard when they catch you leaning. A successful sway also gives you timing priority on your very next move, letting it land first regardless of speed — great for setting up an overhand.

Combos

Landing clean hits builds a damage streak (up to +3) that carries across the whole match. Getting hit by anything other than a jab resets your streak. Jab itself never builds or breaks a streak — it can't carry a fight on its own.

Special: Jab vs Hooks/Body Shots

A jab can't stop a hook or body shot's momentum — if a jab meets one of these, both punches land.

Haymaker

A clean, unguarded overhand cross stuns the opponent's next beat — if they'd picked Guard they still cover up fine, otherwise their move fails.

Health & Rounds

Each fighter has 30 HP, and recovers 30% of remaining HP (rounded down) between rounds. A KO (0 HP) ends the match instantly. Otherwise, each round is scored on damage dealt, accuracy, and shots landed — whoever wins more rounds wins the match, even if they lost a round badly.
