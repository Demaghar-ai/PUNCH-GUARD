A simple 2-player boxing mind game.
Pick 3 moves, outguess your opponent.

How to Play

The Basics

A match is 3 rounds. Each round is 3 conflicts. Each conflict, both players secretly pick 3 moves, then they clash one beat at a time. Each fighter has 30 HP.

Speed & Damage

When both attack, the faster move lands and the slower one misses. Equal speed usually means both land. A few pairs behave differently — jab can't stop a hook or overhand's momentum (both land), and two matching power punches from opposite hands (like Left Hook vs Right Hook) clash instead of trading in full.

Guard & Sway

Guard blocks ordinary punches outright, but heavy shots (uppercuts, overhand) still chip some damage through. Sway dodges straight punches for free, but against hooks, uppercuts, and overhand you have to lean the correct way — guess wrong and you eat it hard. A good sway also sets up your very next strike to land first.

Chains

Stringing together clean hits and good reads builds two bonuses for your next strike: extra damage (Power) and extra speed (Speed). Get hit or guard up and the bonus is gone — keep the pressure on to keep it going.

Stun & Knockdown

A big enough hit rocks your opponent, and their next move just fails — unless they guard or sway it off. Getting dropped to 0 HP isn't the end either — you get back up and keep fighting, though repeated knockdowns raise the risk of a fight-ending knockout.

Winning

HP recovers a bit between rounds. The match is decided by who wins more rounds, not just total damage — so a bad round doesn't sink you if you take the other two. Either player can also surrender to end the match early as a No Contest.
